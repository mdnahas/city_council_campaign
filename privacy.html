Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Privacy Policy

The Michael Nahas Campaign runs this website. The website collects information you volunteer and collects information in hidden ways. Mike Nahas values your privacy, but the campaign operates in a world with limited choices.

Any of that information will be passed on to governments when legally required. There are lots of legal requirements on campaigns.

The website will collect the information you volunteer, such as name, email address or phone number. That information may be passed on to others. We try to limit what 3rd parties can do, but some things may be out of our control.

The website may also collect information in hidden ways, like set cookies or collect analytic information about the webpages you use. The campaign does that to provide services and measure how the website is doing. We will use 3rd party services to collect the analytic information and, while we try to limit what 3rd parties can do, some things may be out of our control.

Terms of Service

You are required to play nice. You are not allowed to mess with the website in any way. You are not allowed to mess with anyone who uses the website in any way.
